# SuperMoney, Novae, and Mesa Group Consulting’s Affiliate Pages

## Executive summary

Mesa Group Consulting appears to be using SuperMoney as a comparison-and-referral partner for the five pages labelled SuperMoney affiliate, with Mesa attracting the visitor and SuperMoney routing that visitor to third-party card issuers or lenders.

Mesa’s own disclosures and live personal-loan, auto-refinance, and student-loan-refinance implementations support that conclusion, including affiliate-style tracking on the student-loan-refinance link.[1][2][3][4] The Novae on the board is most likely Novae, LLC, doing business as Novae · The Credit and Money Company, which distributes consumer and small-business financial solutions through its programs and outside providers.[5][6][7] The five Novae labels fit a second referral or distribution relationship for debt help, estate-planning and insurance-related offers, and business debt or credit services, but the board alone does not establish that each proposed page is live or disclose its exact commercial terms.[8][7] In both arrangements, Mesa is likely the branded education and lead-generation layer rather than the lender, insurer, card issuer, or ultimate service provider.[9][10][11]

## SuperMoney: offerings and referral role

**Reporting date: November 19, 2026. Evidence key —** The undated Miro labels supplied by the client show proposed page assignments, not live-page status. SuperMoney’s company documents establish its available marketplace and publisher tools; Mesa’s own disclosures and live pages establish narrower, observable Mesa implementations.[12][9][13][14][10]

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based arrangement in which Mesa may be compensated when a client enrolls, buys, or completes a specified action with a third-party partner. **Lead generation** is the acquisition of potential customers for a provider; SuperMoney describes an advertiser service that supplies verified contact information for potential customers and lets advertisers target audiences. A **product provider** is the bank, lender, issuer, insurer, or other business that actually supplies a financial product and makes the relevant credit decision. A **comparison marketplace** is a service where consumers compare rates, reviews, and possible eligibility across products supplied by other businesses. A **referral partner** is a business that directs its audience to another company’s products through tracked links or other tools and can earn a commission when users find products through it.[10][15][9][16][13][14]

Judgment — SuperMoney can sit between Mesa and underlying banks, lenders, card issuers, and other providers. Rather than Mesa building its own lender network or originating credit, a Mesa page can educate a visitor, present a comparison tool or link, and route the visitor into SuperMoney’s offer-comparison process.[9][13][14]

### What does SuperMoney do?

SuperMoney is a U.S. personal-finance platform that combines a money-management app—account tracking, automated budgeting, credit monitoring, and its Sense AI assistant—with a comparison marketplace covering 50+ financial-product categories. For Mesa’s five proposed pages, the relevant marketplace categories include credit cards and personal loans.[9]

The app is not merely a directory of financial offers. SuperMoney says users can view transactions and accounts in one place, receive a budget based on actual spending, see assets, liabilities and cash flow, analyze spending, obtain debt-strategy and savings guidance, and build a financial plan with Sense AI. It also advertises a credit-score feature with an improvement plan.[17] SuperMoney says the app has a paid premium tier after a free trial, alongside a free version with fewer features.[9][18]

Its other principal role is a **comparison marketplace**: a service that helps a consumer see, compare, and select financial products supplied by other businesses. SuperMoney describes itself as a financial-services comparison platform and says consumers can compare rates, read reviews, and see what they may qualify for. Its public categories include banking, credit cards, debt help, insurance, loans, mortgages, and refinancing.[19][16]

Its site navigation includes balance-transfer, travel, cashback, rewards, introductory-APR, low-interest, no-annual-fee, secured, bad-credit, airline, and business credit cards. [20][21]

- **Personal loans:** SuperMoney’s personal-loans area includes a quote engine labelled Apply & Pre-Qualify and Get Competing Personal Loan Offers. ### Is SuperMoney a product provider

SuperMoney is a combination of a consumer-app provider, a comparison marketplace, and a referral/monetization intermediary. It provides its own app features and paid app subscription, but it generally does not provide the underlying loans, credit cards, deposit accounts, or insurance products displayed in its marketplace.[9][18][31]

For lending, SuperMoney states plainly that it is not a lender: it does not make or service loans and does not decide who receives approval. Instead, third-party lenders review customer requests and make their own credit decisions.[9] Its debt-consolidation materials likewise say that the website operator does not broker loans to lenders, make loans, or make credit decisions; it submits user-provided information to lenders, which then decide whether to approve an application.[26]

On a personal-loan funnel, SuperMoney additionally says its website operator is not a loan broker or lender agent and that submitted information is passed to a lender.[104]

SuperMoney also says it does not act as a lender’s agent or representative.[9][104]

The same distinction applies to bank accounts. SuperMoney says it is not a bank, does not hold deposits, and that a checking or savings account found through the site is opened with the bank that offers it.[9] It also says it is not a credit-repair company, debt-settlement company, or financial adviser; its stated role is to give users information and tools while leaving decisions to them.[9]

A **pre-qualification** is an early assessment of whether a consumer may qualify for an offer. It is not final credit approval. SuperMoney says its loan process can show pre-qualified rates from multiple lenders through one form, while stressing that pre-qualification does not ensure loan approval and that a selected lender may request additional information before deciding.[9][25][26] For debt-consolidation offers, SuperMoney says one or more soft credit inquiries may be used to check rates and terms, while proceeding with a lending partner can require a hard credit inquiry that may affect the customer’s credit.[26]

Its auto-refinance and student-refinance pages make the same practical division of roles. SuperMoney presents the comparison interface, while its lending partners provide and decide on the offers. It says rate checking involves a soft pull in these flows, but a consumer who proceeds to a lender should treat the later full application as a separate stage.[23][24]

SuperMoney’s own commercial model reinforces this distinction. It says it earns revenue through commissions from financial institutions when consumers take products found through the service, app-subscription fees, and website advertising. It says some—but not all—institutions featured on the site compensate it, and compensation can affect which products appear and their order, although SuperMoney says it does not affect its ratings, reviews, or published facts.[18]

One third-party affiliate listing uses broad language saying SuperMoney offers mortgage products. That should be read as marketplace availability, not loan origination, because SuperMoney’s own disclosures say third-party lenders make lending decisions and provide the actual loans.[32][9]

**In ordinary language, SuperMoney supplies the shopping and offer-routing system, plus its own finance app; banks, lenders, card issuers, and other providers supply the financial products.** That distinction prevents a visitor from mistaking a comparison page or a rate-check form for a direct Mesa or SuperMoney loan application.[9][18]

In the marketplace journey, a consumer supplies basic personal and product-need information, receives potential provider choices, and can generate offers from selected providers; the loan, account-opening, or insurance transaction then takes place on the provider’s website.[33] For its loan offer engine, SuperMoney says a consumer submits one application through direct integrations with lending partners and can receive pre-approved offers from several online lenders, then select the lender with the preferred rates and terms.[34][23] SuperMoney describes this as direct lender matching rather than a lead auction that sells personal data to the highest bidder.[23][34]

SuperMoney’s advertiser terms say its qualification forms collect prospective customers’ information, computerized filters match that information against advertisers’ criteria, and SuperMoney obtains authorization before transmitting it to an advertiser.[105]

SuperMoney’s advertiser terms say computerized filters compare a consumer’s preferences and qualification-form answers with an advertiser’s evaluation criteria; with the consumer’s permission, SuperMoney then forwards the information to that advertiser. The terms define a “Consumer Request” to include a click, data lead, call, quote request, or other referral type specified in the commercial agreement.[105]

SuperMoney’s advertiser terms say its qualification forms use computerized filters to compare consumers’ information with advertisers’ evaluation criteria, obtain authorization to forward that information, and transmit it to the advertiser. The terms define a “Consumer Request” to include a click, data lead, call, quote request, or other referral form.[105]

SuperMoney’s advertiser agreement identifies SuperMoney, LLC as the operator of SuperMoney.com, the SuperMoney Network affiliate network, and related advertising technology. It defines the network as third-party affiliates, publishers, and other partners that promote advertisers’ offers.[105]

[Super Links](https://www.supermoney.com/monetize/super-links) is the most direct explanation for the phrase SuperMoney affiliate on the board. SuperMoney describes it as its affiliate network for publishers that want to monetize financial content. Its Simple Offer Links let a publisher use a provided tracking link in an article or page, send readers to a relevant offer or comparison destination, and earn a commission.[9][13] SuperMoney says audience partners earn commissions when their users find products through them.[14]

SuperMoney also offers co-branded alternative-offer pages for publishers whose primary offer does not itself pay out.[13]

Money Marketplace is an embedded comparison experience: instead of sending visitors immediately away from the publisher’s website, the publisher can place comparison content or tools under its own brand. The Pre-qualification Platform is more interactive. The platform describes a business-assisted pathway as well: first, the business fills out a loan application with the client through a SuperMoney portal; second, it sends the application to the client by email or text for review and submission; third, business and client can review resulting offers together. SuperMoney also advertises CRM pre-filling, shared viewing of offers, and reporting that distinguishes pre-qualified from non-qualified users.[37] That is a possible operating model for a consulting firm, but it is not necessary for a straightforward affiliate-link page.

### What is Evert likely using SuperMoney for?

The most likely use is customer acquisition and referral: Mesa attracts a visitor through a branded page about a financial need, offers comparison-oriented educational material or a rate-check prompt, and then directs that visitor into SuperMoney’s marketplace or lender-offer flow. That use fits Mesa’s stated role as a comparison, education, and referral service rather than a lender.[8][11]

This is not solely an inference from the Miro-board labels. Mesa’s [affiliate disclosure](https://www.mesagroupconsulting.com/affiliate-disclosure) identifies SuperMoney as a partner company and says Mesa may recommend partner products and receive a commission or referral fee when a customer enrolls.[43] Mesa’s [disclaimer](https://www.mesagroupconsulting.com/disclaimer) specifically describes SuperMoney as handling comparisons for personal loans, auto and student refinancing, debt consolidation, and credit cards—the same five categories assigned to SuperMoney on the board.[1]

Mesa’s public disclosures provide direct evidence of how the commercial relationship is meant to work. Its affiliate disclosure says that when a customer uses SuperMoney through Mesa referral links or secures a loan or product, Mesa may receive a commission. The disclosure says compensation comes from the partner rather than the customer and that the stated customer price is the same whether the customer reaches the partner through Mesa or directly.[10][43] This establishes a referral model, not a disclosed commission amount, payment trigger, or signed-contract term.

Mesa’s privacy policy also describes SuperMoney as a source of financial-product comparisons and says Mesa may share basic information with SuperMoney for recommendations and comparisons. It separately says lenders reached through SuperMoney and other lending networks may receive information needed for loan applications and pre-qualification.[44] That is consistent with a visitor being handed from Mesa’s customer-facing funnel into SuperMoney and then, with the visitor’s participation, into a lender’s own process.

Mesa’s live [student-loan-refinancing page](https://www.mesagroupconsulting.com/student-loan-refi) supplies the clearest category-specific example. It displays SuperMoney branding beside a rate-check call to action, tells visitors they can compare multiple lenders, and links the Check Your Rate button directly to SuperMoney’s [student-loan-refinance application](https://app.supermoney.com/student-loan-refinance/?offer_id=1629&aff_id=1187&aff_sub=2815&utm_source=Partner-2815&utm_medium=affiliate&network=partner).[4] The link includes `aff_id=1187`, `aff_sub=2815`, `utm_medium=affiliate`, and `network=partner`, which are referral-tracking parameters consistent with an affiliate or partner link.[4]

### Synthesis

SuperMoney is best understood as three related businesses in one: a money-management app, a financial-product comparison marketplace, and an affiliate/publisher platform. Its consumer marketplace routes users toward third-party providers, while its Super Links, co-branded pages, widgets, and pre-qualification tools let publishers monetize that route.[9][13][14]

For Mesa, the pattern is already visible: Mesa names SuperMoney as a partner, describes it as the comparison source for all five board topics, and uses affiliate-tracked or branded SuperMoney links on live loan-refinancing pages.[43][1][3][4] The strongest conclusion is that Evert is using—or planning to use—SuperMoney to turn topic pages into comparison-and-referral journeys. Underlying lenders and issuers supply and approve the products; Mesa’s possible payment depends on the partner arrangement and qualifying customer action.[9][10][11]

## Novae: identity, offerings and partners

Novae is not a single-product lender or insurer. It is a financial-services distribution business: it markets its own branded programs, brings customers to outside providers, and recruits affiliates and co-branded sites to generate leads and sales. That distinction matters because a page branded or labelled “Novae” may lead to a Novae program, a lender network, an estate-planning platform, or an insurance-comparison partner rather than to a product Novae itself underwrites.

### Which Novae does the board refer to?

The best-evidenced match is the financial-services business at `novaemoney.com`, which Novae LLC says it operates and which is branded **Novae · The Credit and Money Company**.[6][5] Novae LLC says that it operates that site, and the site describes its mission as increasing access to credit, capital, entrepreneurship, and other financial solutions for consumers and small businesses.[6][5]

Novae identifies itself as a fintech company—financial technology company—serving consumers and small businesses nationwide. Its published materials say it was founded in 2014, serves all 50 states and Puerto Rico, and lists a Conyers, Georgia address for Novae, LLC. A 2024 company announcement likewise calls it Novae - The Credit & Money Company and describes it as a Black-owned fintech company serving consumers and small businesses nationwide.[49][50][51]

Mesa Group’s public materials support this match because Mesa identifies Novae as a third-party partner/provider and describes Novae as serving business funding and personal and business debt relief.[8][7] Mesa lists Novae among its “Key Third-Party Partners” and among provider services accessible through Mesa’s website or services.[8][11] More specifically, Mesa calls Novae a provider of “business funding and debt relief for business and personal” matters, and says it may share business and personal information with Novae for funding applications and debt-relief services.[7]

This is not **Res Novae Group**, which is a different entity associated with `resnovaegroup.com`, nor the former **Novae Group plc**, established in 1986, a London/Bermuda property-and-casualty, marine, aviation, and political-risk insurance and reinsurance business operating through Lloyd’s Syndicate 2007.[52][53] Neither alternative fits Mesa’s stated funding and debt-relief relationship or the consumer and small-business service catalogue.

**Conclusion: the board’s Novae is best identified as Novae, LLC / Novae · The Credit and Money Company, because its credit-and-capital focus and Mesa’s published funding/debt-relief descriptions align directly with the board’s Novae-labelled pages.** Mesa’s partner lists do not give Novae’s corporate name, domain, contract, or page-by-page commercial terms, so the board label does not itself prove a particular agreement.[5][6][7][8]

### What does Novae do?

Novae presents itself as a **financial-services marketplace and partner ecosystem**. In ordinary terms, it is a front door through which consumers and businesses can find financial products and services. Some offerings are Novae-branded programs; others are supplied by lenders, licensed service platforms, insurers, or specialist partners. Novae says it seeks to operate as a one-stop source of financial solutions through a combination of its own programs, established-firm partnerships, and affiliates and direct-sales professionals.[51]

For consumers, Novae’s site lists Debt Help, credit cards, personal loans, life insurance, auto-loan refinancing, student-loan refinancing, myNovaeCredit Monitoring, and myNovaeDisputes Manager.[49][59] Its consumer credit products are not conventional credit repair: Novae describes them as self-directed software, monitoring, education, and guided steps under the customer’s control, and says it does not work on a customer’s credit for them or promise score increases, deletions, approvals, funding, or a time frame.[59]

For businesses, Novae lists Business Credit, Business Funding, Consumer Financing, and Business Debt Help.[49] Its business-facing site describes the range as capital, credit, and structure for business owners, while its business-services page says Novae and trusted partners offer products and services to startups and established businesses.[60][61] That wording matters: the company may market an offer and manage the customer path without being the regulated lender, insurer, or legal-document provider.

Novae explicitly says it is not a lender for personal-loan offers. Rather, prospective borrowers use its site to apply for pre-qualification or pre-approval through a network of lenders, and the final application is completed directly with a lender.[62] Novae’s terms similarly say it does not originate, underwrite, fund, service, or guarantee loans; those decisions and terms belong to the independent lender.[6] In affiliate marketing, this is a **referral or marketplace model**: the site introduces a prospective customer to the provider that may actually deliver the financial product.

Novae’s own disclosures also say that Novae LLC and its affiliates can receive commissions per sale or per lead for credit-card offers, loan offers, and other services shown on `novaemoney.com`; it may also list some credit-card offers for which it earns no commission.[6] A **lead** is a prospective customer who submits information or begins an application; a **sale** normally means a completed enrolment, product purchase, or funded transaction, depending on the arrangement.

The five board labels assigned to Novae do not all involve the same underlying service. The following distinctions are central.

- **Debt relief:** Novae’s consumer **Debt Help** program is aimed at specified unsecured debts—debts not backed by collateral—such as credit-card debt, payday loans, medical debt, private student loans, personal loans, lines of credit, personal business debts, and auto repossessions. [63] Novae says it offers the program with a licensed debt-resolution-services platform. It assists the client in establishing a dedicated savings account under the client’s control; the client deposits money monthly, and negotiators seek settlements with creditors. Novae says it does not assume debts, make the client’s ordinary creditor payments, or provide tax, bankruptcy, accounting, legal, or credit-repair services. [63]

Novae says its consumer Debt Help program may in some cases involve The Law Firm of Higbee & Associates, doing business as Advantage Law.[63]

- **Trust & will plan:** Novae’s broader catalogue has listed Will Estate Plan and Trust Estate Plan among consumer products. [12] Novae’s August 2024 compensation plan lists Will & Trust as a Novae product or service, and Novae-associated co-brand pages route visitors to Trust & Will for the underlying estate-plan documents. [64][65] A reported Novae Trust & Will flow lists wills at $199 for an individual and $299 for couples, with first-year membership included and $19 per year thereafter; trusts are listed at $499 for an individual and $599 for couples, with first-year membership included and $39 per year thereafter. [12] The commercially relevant downstream provider is Trust & Will, an estate-planning document platform for wills, trusts, healthcare directives, and powers of attorney. [10] In this service line, Novae is best understood as a possible marketing or referral layer, not as the legal-document provider.

- **Life insurance:** The `novaemoney.com` page is titled **Life Insurance – Novae**. Novae presents life insurance as an opportunity to compare coverage options, not as insurance it issues itself. [66][59] Its life-insurance page says it partners with Policygenius so visitors can compare policies; after a visitor selects a policy, a Policygenius agent prepares the application paperwork for review and e-signature. [66] The insurance carrier makes the coverage offer after underwriting, the insurer’s evaluation of risk and eligibility.

Novae’s links to Policygenius carry affiliate and referral identifiers.[108][109][110]

- **Business debt relief:** Novae’s **Business Debt Help** is a separate debt-restructuring service for business debts. Its page says it works with a licensed debt-restructuring platform, and it accepts a broad set of liabilities: cash advances, taxes, lawsuits, vendor debts, credit-card debts, judgments, supplier debts, loans, leases, secured debts, and debts with personal guarantees. [67] A personal guarantee is a business owner’s promise to repay a business debt personally if the business does not pay.

Novae says its Business Debt Help program does not assume the business’s debt.[111]

- **Business credit:** Novae markets a business-credit-building program named **Lendavo**. [68] It aims to help an owner establish credit under the business’s own name, understand its funding readiness, and use vendor and lender resources to seek future credit-line opportunities. [68][60] It is not a loan or grant from Novae.

Consumer Debt Help and Business Debt Help are especially important not to conflate. Consumer Debt Help concerns qualifying unsecured personal debts and uses a savings-and-settlement model. Novae says a person may qualify with at least $10,000 of debt, while its FAQ says total unsecured debt should exceed $10,000 and the person must live in a state where the service is available.[63] The program is not available in all states; one booking page lists Oregon, Colorado, West Virginia, and Rhode Island as exclusions.[63][69]

Debt relief is also not debt consolidation. Novae defines **debt consolidation** as obtaining a new loan to combine multiple debts into one fixed monthly payment. It defines **debt relief** as creditor negotiation intended to reduce the outstanding balance through settlements.[63] This means a Mesa debt-relief page labelled Novae would likely lead to a settlement/restructuring consultation, whereas a debt-consolidation page would normally lead toward a loan marketplace or lender application.

Novae’s Debt Help page gives operational detail and material risk disclosures. It says program fees range from 14% to 25% of enrolled debt, vary by state, are not charged upfront, and are paid after debts are resolved.[63] It says programs range from 20 to 48 months, the first settlement averages four to seven months after enrolment, and later settlements occur approximately every three to six months from the prior settlement.[63] Novae reports that customers who make all monthly program payments save approximately 40% to 50% of enrolled debt, averaging 43%, on completion before program fees; it also says results are not guaranteed and not all clients complete the program.[63]

The stated risks are substantive. Novae warns that debt resolution will likely adversely affect credit, may lead to collection activity or lawsuits, may allow interest and fees to accrue, and can create tax consequences if debt is forgiven.[63] A customer who submits the booking form consents to sharing contact information with Novae, affiliates, partners, and authorized third parties for calls or texts about debt-relief or debt-consolidation services.

Novae states, “I do not have to consent to receive these types of calls or text messages as a condition of purchasing any goods or services.”[69]

Business Debt Help follows a different structure. Novae says it is for businesses with at least $15,000 in debt, begins with a free no-obligation consultation, and accepts only business debts.[67] It describes taking the business’s affordable monthly budget and using it to negotiate creditor settlements or payment terms, ranging from pennies-on-the-dollar settlements to payment in full.[67] Novae states a $75 start-up cost per creditor; further fees depend on the debt reduction achieved and the time gained to repay, and are paid over time from monthly program funding without interest.[67]

Novae says Business Debt Help is intended for owners who cannot afford present payments but want to repay at a realistic pace, seek to protect assets without bankruptcy, and do not want more high-rate borrowing.[67] It says completion usually takes two to four years, sometimes faster and sometimes slower; clients who completed the program paid back 89% of their debt over an average 35 months, including the cost of services.[67] It also warns that clients may have to sacrifice credit, may face collections or lawsuits, may see debt grow through fees and interest, and may owe tax on forgiven debt.[67]

Novae’s Business Debt Help page advertises both an average 80% reduction in client payments and reductions of up to 90%.[67] Those are different measures—a reported average and an upper-end promotional claim—and the page does not give a period or method that reconciles them. The usable conclusion is that Novae markets substantial payment reduction, not that any individual business will obtain either result.

Business Credit has a different economic purpose from Business Debt Help. Lendavo DIY costs **$995 one time** and includes platform access, a step-by-step credit-building system, a vendor and lender database, coaching and support, live Q&A calls, and ongoing accountability. Lendavo Guided costs **$2,495 one time** and adds a coached route described as positioning the customer for $50,000 or more in credit-line opportunities.[68]

Novae expressly says that its Business Credit program positions customers to apply for $50K+ in credit lines, but funding is not guaranteed; lenders determine approval, amounts, and terms, and the program is not a loan or grant.[68] It says a participant needs a registered business entity and, even without revenue, must have funds to open vendor accounts, make small purchases, and repay them on time so they report.[68] Its stated timeline is vendor-account reporting in 30–60 days and larger lines in six to 12 months depending on consistency; applications without a personal guarantee may become possible after several accounts report, usually in six to 12 months.[68]

**Conclusion: Novae is a mixed-model financial-services distributor—part branded program provider, part marketplace, and part referral channel—rather than a universal lender, insurer, or legal-services firm.** The debt-relief, business-debt-relief, and business-credit board labels map to distinct products with distinct customer risks, eligibility, and economics.[63][67][68][61]

Novae’s stated average 43% reduction before fees, less its stated 14%–25% fee range, produces an implied net debt reduction of **18%–29% of enrolled debt**.[63]

Lendavo Guided’s $2,495 one-time price is **$1,500** and **150.8% higher** than Lendavo DIY’s $995 one-time price.[68]

Novae’s August 2024 compensation plan calls the business model “Affiliate Marketing”: affiliates refer customers to Novae products and services, while Novae handles customer service, delivery, billing, and technical operations.[64]

The catalogue in that plan expressly includes Consumer Financial Products—high-yield savings accounts, auto and student loan refinancing, credit cards, and money-market savings accounts—alongside Will & Trust, Novae Debt Help, Business Debt Help, and the NM Business Credit Finance Suite and DIY version.[64]

Novae also sells myNovaeDisputes Manager as AI-powered, self-directed credit-dispute software. ### Synthesis

Novae LLC is the Novae that fits Mesa’s board: a consumer-and-small-business fintech distributor with a catalogue spanning debt help, business debt help, business credit, lending access, estate-planning referrals, and insurance comparison.[5][6][7] The evidence shows several different delivery models beneath that single brand: licensed debt-resolution platforms for consumer and business debt help, Trust & Will for estate documents, Policygenius and insurers for life insurance, lenders for credit products, and Lendavo for paid business-credit building.[63][67][66][10][68]

A typical SuperMoney loan path has three stages:

1. **First, the visitor supplies basic information.** For debt-consolidation loans, SuperMoney describes a single online form through which a customer submits information so lenders in its network can evaluate the request. [26][25]

Its homepage says it works with trusted names including SmartCredit, SuperMoney, and Novae, while its terms list both companies as third-party providers whose services may be accessed through Mesa’s website or services.[79][8] That is stronger than the board alone: Mesa publicly presents the companies as part of its partner ecosystem.

There is direct evidence that all five SuperMoney-labelled topics moved from the board’s intended structure to tracked referral routes. Mesa’s personal-loan, auto-loan-refinancing, and student-loan-refinancing pages contain SuperMoney outbound links with affiliate-style and partner-style tracking parameters; its credit-card and debt-consolidation-loan pages do as well.[2][3][4][45][46] Mesa’s student-refinancing page additionally says it may earn a referral fee when a customer completes a loan, and the debt-consolidation page says it may earn a referral fee when a visitor completes a loan, in each case at no extra cost to the customer.[4][46]

Mesa’s personal-loan page has a comparable SuperMoney destination with referral parameters.[2] The Miro label for credit cards is also commercially plausible because a SuperMoney credit-cards affiliate programme is listed as active, with a stated payout of up to $50 CPA—cost per acquisition, a payment triggered by a defined acquired customer action.[38] But that listed programme is evidence of an available opportunity, not a statement of Mesa’s rate or an assurance that Mesa joined it.

The SuperMoney affiliate materials do not publicly state a single current payout schedule or identify one universal payment trigger across all products.[12] Accordingly, Mesa’s public tracked links and disclosures show an operating referral path and potential commission, while Mesa does not publish a payment amount for an individual click, application, approval, funded loan, or completed sale.[2][3][4][10]

SuperMoney’s rates are campaign-specific.[105]

The Novae labels need a more service-specific reading. Novae itself has affiliate, co-brand, and white-label structures, any of which could support a Mesa-branded financial-services page.[12][6] Mesa’s own disclosure specifically connects Novae to possible commissions after funding is secured or debt relief is enrolled in.[10] That supports an active commercial referral concept, rather than merely a casual mention of a company name.

There is, however, an important difference within the Novae cluster. Mesa’s privacy policy identifies PolicyGenius and various life-insurance carriers for life-insurance referrals, and Trust & Will for estate-planning documents.[44] Novae’s relevant service pages also route life-insurance customers to Policygenius and estate-planning customers to Trust & Will.[50][12] **The board’s Novae tags therefore most naturally describe Novae as Mesa’s upstream marketing or service-platform partner, while Policygenius and Trust & Will may be the downstream providers delivering the actual insurance or document service.**[12][44][50]

Mesa’s terms reinforce the underlying role division. Mesa says it is not a lender, does not make approval decisions, and does not set rates, fees, or underwriting standards; it connects clients with third-party lenders and providers.[1] SuperMoney says lenders make loan decisions, and Novae’s flows often direct customers onward to a lender, insurer, debt-resolution platform, or other provider.[9][62][6] The board therefore indicates a planned acquisition-and-referral system, not a plan for Mesa to originate all ten underlying products.

Mesa’s Business Credit Builder page supplies direct evidence of a Mesa–Novae operational connection: Mesa says its Business Credit Experts work in conjunction with Novae to assist clients with several funding approvals across funding tiers.[80] That page implements a business-credit offering corresponding to the board’s Novae-labelled business-credit category.[80][12]

Mesa’s public affiliate disclosure also identifies both SuperMoney and Novae as partner companies, says Mesa may receive a commission or referral fee when a customer enrols with a partner, and says the partner—not Mesa—provides the product.[103]

### Synthesis —

For any Mesa page, the practical checks are its live Mesa URL, the destination URL reached by its call to action, and the terms and privacy policy governing the provider service at that destination.[8][93][2][3][4] For each other board-labelled route, the concrete evidence needed is the operating Mesa URL, its outbound destination, and the partner terms governing that destination.[2][3][4][12][10]

Mesa states that it may receive a commission when a customer uses its SuperMoney referral links or secures a loan or product, and when a customer secures funding or enrols in debt relief through Novae.[10] The Miro board shows Evert’s intended page-to-partner structure, while live tracked links show that at least part of the SuperMoney side has been implemented.[12][3][4]

1. Treat each Mesa page as a referral page unless it expressly identifies Mesa as the direct provider.
2. Distinguish rate checks and pre-qualification from final lender approval.
3. Treat debt consolidation loans and debt relief as different products with different customer consequences.
4. Before relying on a listed offer, review the destination provider’s current price, eligibility, privacy, and credit-impact disclosures.

### Open questions

The exact contract terms, commission amounts, qualifying events, and active status of each of the ten proposed Mesa pages remain open. The precise Novae provider or product path for the trust and will plan and life-insurance pages, and whether Mesa has launched those specific offerings, also remain open. A live Mesa page, its destination and disclosures, or the applicable Mesa-partner agreement would settle each page’s implementation and compensation model.
